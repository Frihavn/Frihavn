THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

A few quick answers before you choose your next wall piece.

What does the size mean?+

Sizes are listed in maps wide by maps tall. For example, 2 × 2 maps means the artwork is assembled from four in-game map tiles.

Can I request a custom design?+

Absolutely. Send us your idea, preferred dimensions, and any references through the community link. We’ll discuss what’s possible.

How do I get the artwork in-game?+

Once availability and price are confirmed, we’ll arrange an in-game pickup or delivery. The exact details depend on your server.

Can I use the artwork outside the game?+

Please ask before reposting or using artwork elsewhere. Some designs may be inspired by characters or works owned by their respective creators.
